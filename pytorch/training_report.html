Training report · llm-from-scratch

gpt2ish on the full TinyStories corpus

A 100.7M-parameter decoder (RoPE + GQA + SwiGLU, tied embeddings) trained from scratch on all 2,119,719 TinyStories examples, on a single RTX 4070 SUPER — two full epochs, each its own fresh warmup + cosine decay rather than one schedule stretched across both. Epoch 1 ran eager (bf16 autocast); epoch 2 loaded epoch 1's weights via --init_from and ran under torch.compile.
