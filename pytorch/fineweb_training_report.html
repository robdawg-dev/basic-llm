Training report · llm-from-scratch

fineweb on FineWeb-Edu

A 113.3M-parameter decoder (RoPE + GQA + SwiGLU, tied embeddings) trained from scratch on 1.9B tokens of FineWeb-Edu, tokenized with SmolLM2's 49,152-vocab tokenizer. Single RTX 4070 SUPER, torch 2.14.0+cu132, --compile --fused_ce (native nn.LinearCrossEntropyLoss). Under one epoch over the sampled corpus — FineWeb-Edu is large and deduplicated enough that less than a full pass is the norm, unlike the TinyStories runs before it.
